Academic Mission & Background

About Peregrine Academy

Pioneering research-grounded pedagogy for Spoken Emirati (Gulf) Arabic fluency.

Our Mission

Peregrine Academy is an educational initiative dedicated to bridging the communication gap between expatriates, international professionals, scholars, and native Emirati communities in the United Arab Emirates and the broader Gulf region. While Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) serves formal literature and journalism, genuine social, commercial, and governmental rapport in the Gulf requires command of authentic Spoken Emirati Arabic (al-Lahja al-Imārātiyya).

Our mission is to dismantle the barriers to dialectal Arabic fluency by providing structured, phonetically accurate, and culturally authentic learning tools designed for practical, real-world mastery.

Academic Direction

All curriculum, dialectal transcriptions, native recordings, and pedagogical frameworks at Peregrine Academy are directed by Dr. Muhammed Swalih T (PhD, The English and Foreign Languages University - EFLU, Hyderabad). With extensive academic scholarship in Arabic linguistics, Gulf dialectology, and second-language acquisition, Dr. Swalih integrates classical linguistic precision with modern digital pedagogy.

Our Core Educational Offerings

Peregrine Academy delivers three synchronized educational products:

  • Spoken Arabic LMS Course (30 Contact Hours): A comprehensive, video-guided online curriculum credit-aligned with National Higher Education Qualification Framework (NHEQF) guidelines. Features structured grammatical scaffolding, cultural etiquette in the UAE, high-frequency syntax labs, and continuous progress assessments.
  • Spoken Arabic Book (Digital Reader): A narrative-based digital reader comprising 40 real-life situational chapters and 952 synchronized, crystal-clear audio recordings from native Emirati speakers, paired with word-by-word glosses and linguistic commentary.
  • AI Speaking Partner (Rashid & Maryam): An experimental conversational AI companion grounded directly in textbook vocabulary and real dialogue scenarios, offering safe, non-judgmental conversational practice.

Business Structure & Governance

Peregrine Academy operates as the specialized language and academic training division of Peregrine Training & Consulting (Peregrine T&C), established to foster intercultural communication and professional language readiness.

All digital commerce, checkout transactions, and student enrollments on this storefront (peregrineacademy.in) are processed transparently under strict consumer protection, data privacy, and secure financial processing standards.
