Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 • Effective Date: October 28, 2026

1. Pure Digital Delivery (No Physical Shipping)

Peregrine Academy (operated by Peregrine Training & Consulting) provides 100% digital educational products and online services. We do not dispatch physical packages, parcels, printed bound books, or physical optical media via postal mail or courier.

Shipping Cost: ₹0.00 (Free Electronic Delivery Worldwide)

All products purchased on our storefront are fulfilled digitally and automatically to your registered email address and online browser session.

2. Product Fulfillment Methods & Platforms

Each product tier is delivered electronically through dedicated educational infrastructure:

Spoken Arabic Book (Digital Reader)

Instant Claim Token

Delivery Mode: A unique cryptographic access claim token is generated and delivered immediately on the order confirmation screen and sent via email.

Access Portal: Activated at arabic.peregrineacademy.in. Once activated, grants lifetime access across desktop and mobile browsers.

Spoken Arabic LMS Course

Student Portal Account

Delivery Mode: Automated account creation and course enrollment notification dispatched to your registered email address.

Access Portal: Accessible at learn.peregrineacademy.in. Includes 1-year unlimited video lecture streaming, lesson quizzes, and downloadable audio transcripts.

Complete Arabic Learning Package (Bundle)

Combined Fulfillment

Delivery Mode: Delivers both the LMS course dashboard enrollment credentials and the Digital Reader activation token simultaneously upon payment verification.

3. Delivery Timelines & SLA

  • Automated Instant Delivery: Under normal operational circumstances, digital access credentials and tokens are dispatched within 1 to 5 minutes following successful Razorpay payment capture.
  • Confirmation Screen: As soon as the payment callback is verified, your browser is redirected to a confirmation receipt displaying your Order Reference Number and immediate access instructions.
  • Email Confirmation: A detailed receipt containing your access credentials and activation links is transmitted to the email address provided in the checkout form.

4. Troubleshooting Delivery Delays

If you have completed payment through Razorpay and have not received your enrollment email or claim token within 15 minutes:

  1. Check Spam/Junk/Promotions Folders: Automated onboarding emails may occasionally be filtered by spam filters. Please check all mail tabs for messages from hello@peregrinehub.com or admin@peregrineacademy.in.
  2. Verify Email Accuracy: Confirm that the email address entered during checkout was free from typographical errors.
  3. Contact Academic Support: If you cannot locate your access details, email our support desk at hello@peregrinehub.com with your Razorpay payment ID (e.g., pay_...) or mobile number. Our team will verify the payment ledger and manually re-issue your credentials within 24 hours.

5. Technical Requirements for Digital Access

To successfully receive and engage with our digital educational products, students must have:

  • A modern, updated web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge).
  • A stable internet connection capable of streaming standard definition or high definition video (for LMS courses).
  • Audio output capabilities (speakers or headphones) to benefit from the 952 native pronunciation recordings.
  • A valid, accessible email account to receive order updates and security credentials.
