Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 • Effective Date: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Peregrine Academy, operated by Peregrine Training & Consulting (Peregrine T&C) ("we", "us", or "our"), respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, handling, and protection practices across our storefront (https://peregrineacademy.in) and connected learning applications.

We are committed to absolute transparency regarding what information we collect, the explicit technical purposes for which it is used, and how it is secured.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to execute digital order fulfillment and customer support:

  • Customer Name: Required to personalize your student record, generate certificates of completion, and address order receipts.
  • Email Address: Mandatory identifier used to deliver course access credentials, single-use HMAC claim tokens, transaction receipts, and customer service communications.
  • Phone Number (Optional): Voluntarily provided by the customer for order confirmation notifications or WhatsApp support.
  • Technical Transaction Records: Generated server-side order identifiers (PRGN-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX), selected SKU, purchase amount in INR, and fulfillment statuses.

Financial Data & Payment Processing Boundary

We NEVER store, process, or transmit your sensitive payment card details, CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs on Peregrine Academy servers.

All payment transactions are handled directly through Razorpay Software Private Limited via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant secure iframes. Once your payment succeeds, Razorpay returns only cryptographic transaction proof: a Razorpay Order ID (order_...), Payment ID (pay_...), and an HMAC verification signature.

3. Inter-Service Data Sharing for Product Fulfillment

To deliver your purchased educational products across our unified multi-platform ecosystem, our backend communicates securely with specialized sub-applications:

  • Digital Book Reader (arabic.peregrineacademy.in): When you purchase the Digital Book, our backend creates an HMAC-SHA256 cryptographically signed single-use claim token containing your normalized email and internal order reference. The Reader app validates this cryptographic token to unlock the 40 narrative chapters and 952 audio clips on your device.
  • LMS Course Portal (learn.peregrineacademy.in): When you purchase the LMS Course, your name and email are transmitted via an authenticated server-to-server API to provision your student enrollment on our Next.js-powered course dashboard.

We do not share your personal data with any third-party marketing brokers, advertisers, or external lead aggregators.

4. Analytics, Cookies, and Advertising Disclosures

In accordance with digital advertising transparency guidelines, our storefront utilizes the following analytics tools:

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel): We employ the official Meta Pixel code to measure ad performance on Facebook and Instagram. The Pixel tracks anonymous navigation events (such as PageView and ViewContent). Crucially, the Purchase conversion event is strictly gated to verified, captured server responses to prevent inflated or duplicate conversion reporting.
  • Essential Cookies & Local Storage: Used strictly for operational functions, including maintaining selected product packages and preventing duplicate transaction submissions.

You can configure your browser to reject cookies or use browser extensions to block ad pixels without losing access to purchase or read our course materials.

5. Data Storage and Security Controls

We implement defense-in-depth technical and organizational controls to protect customer data:

  • All storefront and API traffic is strictly encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Serverless execution environments strictly isolate API secrets and service-role database keys from client-accessible code.
  • Database tables enforce PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) and require service-role authorization; public or anonymous access is completely revoked.

6. Data Retention and Your Rights

We retain financial transaction logs and proof of payment for the minimum period required by Indian tax and commercial record-keeping laws.

Under applicable data protection principles, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the rectification of any inaccurate personal details.
  • Request the deletion of your student account and access records, subject to statutory retention obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please email our academic privacy desk at hello@peregrinehub.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Services are designed for adult learners, university scholars, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age without verified parental consent. If we become aware that personal data of a minor has been collected inadvertently, we take immediate steps to delete it.
