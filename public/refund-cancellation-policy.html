Consumer Rights & Protections

Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 • Effective Date: October 28, 2026

1. Nature of Our Digital Products

Peregrine Academy offers intangible, irrevocable digital educational goods including online video masterclasses, interactive digital book readers, and downloadable pronunciation audio files. Once digital access credentials or cryptographic claim tokens are delivered and activated, the content is immediately available in full.

We believe in honest, fair business practices that balance student consumer protection with the proprietary nature of intellectual property and digital content.

Our 100% Technical Delivery Guarantee

If your payment is successfully debited and captured by Razorpay, but our servers fail to deliver your digital access credentials, course dashboard enrollment, or book claim token within 24 hours of purchase, and our academic support team is unable to resolve the technical delivery, you are entitled to a full 100% refund of your purchase price without hesitation.

2. Product-Specific Refund Terms

Spoken Arabic LMS Course (₹1,499)

We offer a 48-hour satisfaction evaluation window from the exact timestamp of enrollment. If you find the curriculum unsuitable for your learning goals, you may request a refund within 48 hours, provided you have completed less than 15% of the course modules and have not downloaded assessment materials. Requests made after 48 hours or following module completion exceeding 15% are ineligible for refund.

Spoken Arabic Book (Digital Reader) (₹499)

Due to the immediate, non-revocable nature of digital literature and the instant availability of all 40 narrative chapters and 952 audio clips upon token claim, purchases of the Digital Book are non-refundable once the single-use claim token has been activated on arabic.peregrineacademy.in. If the claim token has not been activated, a refund may be requested within 24 hours of purchase.

Complete Arabic Learning Package (Bundle) (₹1,998)

The Complete Package bundles both the LMS Course and the Digital Book. Refund requests for the bundle are evaluated under the 48-hour LMS evaluation window, provided the Digital Book claim token has not been activated and less than 15% of the LMS video curriculum has been consumed.

3. Duplicate Charges and Billing Anomalies

In the event that an internet disruption, gateway timeout, or accidental multiple submission results in duplicate charges for the same order:

  • We will immediately verify the duplicate payment proofs in our Razorpay gateway ledger.
  • The redundant transaction will be refunded in full (100%) immediately without administrative deduction.
  • Your primary order and access entitlements will remain active and undisturbed.

4. How to Submit a Refund Request

To initiate a formal refund review, please contact our academic support desk by email:

To: hello@peregrinehub.com

Subject: Refund Request - [Your Order Number]

Please Include the Following Details:

  • Full Name registered during checkout.
  • Customer Email Address used for the purchase.
  • Peregrine Order Reference Number (e.g. PRGN-20261028-XXXXXX).
  • Razorpay Payment ID (e.g. pay_...) from your payment receipt.
  • Clear description of the reason for your refund request.

5. Review and Processing Timelines

  • Acknowledgment: Your refund request will be acknowledged via email within 24 business hours.
  • Evaluation & Decision: Our academic desk will review account activity (module completion, token status) and communicate a written decision within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Payment Gateway Remittance: If approved, the refund will be initiated directly via the Razorpay API to your original payment method (Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI account).
  • Bank Processing Window: Funds typically appear on your bank statement within 5 to 7 business days of gateway initiation, depending on your financial institution's processing cycles.

6. Statutory Consumer Protections

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or waive your non-excludable statutory rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (India) or other applicable consumer laws.
