2. Multi-Tier Resolution Procedure

Level 1: Academic Support Helpdesk

For routine issues regarding course login, audio playback, claim token activation, or general questions, please first write to hello@peregrinehub.com. Our support team resolves over 90% of requests within 24 hours.

Level 2: Formal Grievance Escalation

If Level 1 support fails to resolve your issue within 48 hours, or if you have an unaddressed payment dispute, refund contest, or data privacy grievance, you may escalate directly to the Grievance Officer:

  • Send an email to admin@peregrineacademy.in with the subject Grievance Escalation - [Order ID].
  • State your full name, registered email address, transaction reference, and a summary of prior correspondence.

3. Statutory Timelines & SLA

  • Acknowledgment: The Grievance Officer will acknowledge receipt of your formal complaint within 48 hours, providing a unique ticket or reference code.
  • Investigation: Our technical, financial, and academic teams will review gateway ledgers, system logs, and communication threads.
  • Final Redressal: A formal written decision and resolution will be rendered within 15 business days from the date of formal receipt.

4. Types of Grievances Covered

  • Payment & Billing Disputes: Money debited without order confirmation, duplicate debits, or delays in refund processing.
  • Technical Delivery Failures: Failure to receive course enrollment emails or digital book token activation codes.
  • Privacy & Data Rights: Requests for data erasure, data correction, or inquiries regarding personal information storage under our Privacy Policy.
  • Content Integrity: Reporting intellectual property concerns, broken course modules, or curriculum corrections.
