Academic Curriculum

30 Contact Hours of Guided Video Masterclasses

Curriculum overview aligned with National Higher Education Qualification Framework (NHEQF) continuing education credit guidelines. Full 30 contact hours with interactive dialogue labs, lecture videos, and assessments are hosted on the official Next.js Peregrine LMS platform (learn.peregrineacademy.in).

01

Phonetic System & Distinctive Gulf Sound Shifts

Systematic mastery of the phonetic variations between Modern Standard Arabic and Emirati dialect: the shift of Jim to Yaa (masjid → masyid), Qaf to Gaaf or Jim, and Kaf to Chaf (kayfa haluk → chayf halik).

6 Video Lessons • Sound Comparison Lab • Phonetic Drill Workbook
02

The Emirati Majlis: Hospitality, Kinship & Social Nuance

Master the high-stakes cultural protocols of entering, greeting, and participating in an authentic Gulf majlis. Complex greeting formulas, offering and receiving gahwa (Arabic coffee), and customary poetic compliments.

8 Video Lessons • Real-Life Majlis Simulation • Audio Etiquette Guide
03

Workplace, Commerce & Public Affairs in the UAE

Language tailored for executives, diplomats, lawyers, and healthcare professionals. Navigating ministries, contracting terms, commercial negotiations, and building peer relationships with Emirati colleagues.

7 Video Lessons • Executive Dialogue Scripts • Commercial Glossary
04

High-Frequency Emirati Syntax & Conversational Reflex

Internalize the distinctive grammatical markers of Gulf Arabic: dialectal negations (mā... shē), future tense prefixes (ba- and rāyih), continuous aspect markers (gā'id), and colloquial interrogatives (laysh, shō, wayn).

5 Video Lessons • Syntax Quick-Reference Cards • Grammar Exercises
05

Spontaneous Fluency, Gulf Idioms & Capstone Assessment

Master popular proverbs, humorous expressions, and spontaneous conversational transitions. Complete the final speaking assessment to earn your official Peregrine Academy Certificate of Completion.
